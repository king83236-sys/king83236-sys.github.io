Can Foreigners Get Free Legal Aid in Taiwan? Which Cases Are Covered—and Which Are Not?

鄧宇律師（中文） / Free Legal Aid in Taiwan for Foreigners: Eligibility & Cases | Teng Yu

If your employer has failed to pay your wages, you have been in a traffic accident, you have been scammed, you are involved in a criminal case or you are facing a family dispute in Taiwan, you may not know what to do. Where can you turn if you cannot afford to hire a lawyer?

Foreign nationals can also apply for legal aid in Taiwan. Foreign nationals legally residing in Taiwan can generally apply under the relevant rules. People with certain exceptional residence circumstances may also qualify.

Taiwan's Legal Aid Foundation (LAF) offers free legal consultations and, after an application is reviewed and approved, assistance from a lawyer. Not every case is within the scope of legal aid, and applying for formal assistance does not guarantee approval.

1. Which Legal Problems Can LAF Help With, and How Are They Usually Handled?

Below are common legal problems foreign nationals may encounter in Taiwan and the procedures that may be involved.

Legal problem (examples)Usual stepsHow LAF may help
Unpaid wages, unlawful dismissal or occupational injuryKeep pay and employment records → File a complaint with the labor authority or request mediation → Go to court if necessaryAdvice on employment issues, legal documents, mediation or representation in litigation
Scams or misappropriation of propertyPreserve payment records and conversations → Report to the police and cooperate with the investigation → Consider a civil claimLegal consultation, assistance in criminal proceedings within LAF's scope and civil claims
Police notification or suspicion of a criminal offenseCheck the notice, your role and the required date → Police questioning and prosecutorial investigation → Possible court trialCriminal legal advice and defense during investigation or trial
Traffic accident injuries or compensation disputesSeek medical care and preserve evidence → Assess liability and losses → Negotiate, mediate or litigateCivil compensation advice, mediation and qualifying court representation
Divorce, domestic violence or matters involving childrenPrioritize safety → Organize family and identity documents → Follow the appropriate family-law processFamily-law advice, eligible protection-order matters or other family proceedings
Landlord withholding a deposit or an unpaid loanKeep contracts and payment records → Make a demand or seek mediation → Sue if necessaryLegal consultation, document drafting or qualifying civil representation
Residence status, removal from Taiwan or government decisionsCheck the decision and deadlines → Identify the available remedy → Seek administrative review or litigation if necessaryAdministrative-law advice and representation in qualifying proceedings

These are simplified examples. Not every case follows every step. Whether LAF can assist depends on the specific procedure and the review of your case.

For example: If you are scammed, will reporting it to the police get your money back?

Not necessarily. Holding someone criminally responsible and recovering your financial loss are two different legal issues.

Criminal proceedings: holding the offender responsibleCivil proceedings: recovering your money
Preserve evidence and report to the policeOrganize evidence of transfers and losses
Police and prosecutor investigateNegotiate, mediate or bring a civil claim
The prosecutor may bring charges and the case may go to trialSeek compulsory enforcement if needed
Purpose: determine criminal responsibilityPurpose: seek repayment or compensation

The two paths may overlap, for example through settlement in a criminal case, the return of seized property or a civil claim attached to criminal proceedings. But a criminal conviction does not mean you will necessarily recover all your losses.

LAF's scope of assistance differs between criminal and civil proceedings. Approval for one proceeding does not automatically mean other proceedings are covered.

A Free Legal Consultation Is Not the Same as Having a Lawyer Represent You for Free

LAF's free legal consultations generally do not require a means test or a review of your case's merits. For example, an in-person consultation usually lasts 20 minutes.

However, after the consultation, that lawyer does not automatically continue drafting documents, attending court or handling your case.

If you need comprehensive lawyer assistance, you generally must make a separate application for formal legal aid and obtain approval before services can be provided within the approved scope.

3. Which Cases Does LAF Generally Not Cover?

This information is important when deciding whether to apply for LAF assistance or consider hiring a lawyer at your own expense.

Even if you meet the financial eligibility criteria, certain matters or procedures may be excluded because of limits on LAF's scope of assistance.

Under the current Regulations Governing the Scope of Legal Aid, the following restrictions are particularly worth noting:

Case or procedureGeneral LAF ruleWhat can you consider?
Representation in small-claim civil proceedings and related compulsory enforcementGenerally excluded, with exceptions such as matters affecting basic family livelihood or having particular significanceCheck whether an exception applies, or consider hiring a lawyer at your own expense
Representation in private criminal prosecutionsGenerally excluded; exceptions may apply in especially necessary circumstancesCheck any applicable exception or consult a lawyer about self-funded representation
Representation of complainants or informants during criminal trialsGenerally excluded; exceptions apply for certain crime victims and especially necessary circumstancesConfirm the victim's status, the procedural stage and the nature of the case
Certain retrial or extraordinary appeal proceedingsGenerally excluded, subject to exceptions for particular cases and especially necessary circumstancesAsk a lawyer to assess the legal requirements and prospects first
Certain trademark- and patent-related representationGenerally excluded; exceptions may apply in especially necessary circumstancesCheck the aid restrictions or consider self-funded representation

Important: “Generally excluded” does not mean “no exceptions whatsoever.” Where legal aid is particularly necessary, certain cases may still be approved through special review procedures under the rules.

If no exception applies and you want a lawyer to formally represent you, you may need to consider hiring one at your own expense.

If a case is eligible to apply, does that guarantee approval?

No.

Unpaid-wage claims, compensation after traffic accidents, ordinary civil disputes, family matters and criminal defense may fall within the possible scope of aid, but LAF must still consider eligibility, financial circumstances, the facts of the case and the need for assistance.

Eligible to apply: The type of case and proceeding may qualify for assistance, but the application must be reviewed.

Generally excluded: The service is normally unavailable, but statutory exceptions must still be checked.

Not approved: LAF will not assign a legal aid lawyer for that rejected application. The applicant may ask about review of the decision, other assistance or hiring a lawyer at their own expense.

If you are unsure which category your matter falls into, seek initial legal advice before spending a great deal of time preparing an application for a service that may generally be outside LAF's scope.

4. Can Foreign Nationals and Migrant Workers Apply for LAF Assistance?

Foreign nationals may also apply

Taiwan's legal aid system is not restricted to Taiwanese citizens.

Foreign nationals legally residing in Taiwan can generally apply. Certain exceptional circumstances may also qualify, for example where a person loses their right to reside for reasons beyond their control or is a victim or suspected victim of human trafficking.

Some migrant workers are exempt from the ordinary means test

Under Article 13 of the Legal Aid Act, foreign nationals admitted under Article 46, paragraph 1, subparagraphs 8 to 10 of the Employment Service Act may, after signing the required declaration, be presumed to lack sufficient means.

Examples include qualifying workers in manufacturing, construction, marine fishing, domestic work and caregiving.

Certain financially disadvantaged foreign spouses may also qualify for a declaration-based exemption from the ordinary means test.

But remember:

Being exempt from the means test does not exempt your case from review, nor does it guarantee that you will receive a legal aid lawyer.

LAF must still review whether the case meets its requirements for assistance.

5. If You Do Not Speak Chinese, Can LAF Arrange an Interpreter?

LAF has an interpretation system for the application-review process to help applicants who are not familiar with Chinese explain their situations, including during application, review and reconsideration stages.

If you need Vietnamese, Indonesian, Thai, Filipino or another language, explain your language needs when making the appointment and ask whether an interpreter can be arranged.

LAF also offers a way for foreign nationals to book video legal consultations.

However, interpreting support during the application review does not mean free interpretation is automatically provided for every later meeting with a lawyer, police interview or court hearing. Language arrangements for those later procedures must be confirmed separately.

6. How Do You Apply for a Legal Aid Lawyer? What Documents Do You Need?

The application process usually has four steps:

Step 1: Book an application appointment.

You can book with a convenient LAF branch; it does not have to be in the place where the case arose.

LAF nationwide hotline: 412-8518. From a mobile phone, call 02-412-8518 and select option 3 to contact a branch.

You can also book through LAF's official website.

Step 2: Prepare your documents.

Depending on your situation, prepare a passport, Alien Resident Certificate (ARC) or other identification, together with documents relating to the case.

Examples include court notices, police notices, employment contracts, payslips, money-transfer records, photographs and chat records.

If a means test applies, you may also need income and asset records. Those qualifying under special rules should prepare the required declaration or other supporting materials.

Step 3: Complete LAF's review.

The foundation reviews your eligibility, financial situation and case under its procedures.

The result is usually communicated around 3–5 working days after the application is accepted, but the actual time depends on the case and the branch.

Step 4: Check the decision and next steps.

If approved, LAF arranges a lawyer according to the approved scope of assistance.

If not approved, you can ask why and whether you may apply for reconsideration under the rules.

Applying to LAF itself is free of charge. You do not need to pay an application agent, and you do not need to apply through Teng Yu Law Office.

7. What Is the Difference Between a Legal Aid Lawyer and a Lawyer You Hire at Your Own Expense?

The main advantage of legal aid is that it reduces lawyer fees for people who qualify.

However, formally obtaining a legal aid lawyer requires an application, review and assignment process. If you want to begin handling your matter promptly, choose which lawyer will represent you or obtain a service restricted by LAF's scope of assistance, hiring a lawyer at your own expense may be more suitable.

ComparisonLegal aid lawyerLawyer hired at your own expense
When service can beginApplication, review and assignment are requiredContact a lawyer directly and arrange services once both sides agree, without waiting for LAF review and assignment
Eligibility and means testGenerally required, with statutory exceptionsNo need to pass LAF's eligibility or means test
Choice of lawyerYou may request a particular lawyer, but the request is not guaranteedYou may approach the lawyer you choose; representation begins when both sides agree
Scope of servicesLimited by LAF rules and the approved scopeScope can be agreed with the lawyer according to your needs
Lawyer feesFull or partial aid; some contribution may be requiredYou pay the agreed fees
Will the case be accepted?LAF decides under its review rulesThe lawyer assesses the case and both sides must agree to representation

Why Do Some People Choose to Hire a Lawyer at Their Own Expense?

1. You do not have to wait for LAF review and assignment, so services can be arranged sooner.

If you have received a police, prosecutor's office or court notice, or you want to discuss your case promptly, you can contact a lawyer directly to agree on timing and services without first going through the LAF application process.

2. You can choose the lawyer who will actually handle your case.

LAF permits applicants to request a particular legal aid lawyer, but the foundation decides whether to approve that request.

When hiring a lawyer at your own expense, you can speak with your chosen lawyer, understand how the case will be handled and decide together whether to proceed.

3. Services are not restricted by LAF's scope of aid and can be tailored to your needs.

Even if a particular procedure is generally outside LAF's coverage, you can ask a lawyer whether they are willing to take it on a fee-paying basis.

You can also arrange a one-time consultation, document drafting, accompaniment to mediation or full litigation representation, depending on what you need.
